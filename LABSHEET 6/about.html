A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Thoughtful by design.

I’m Alex Morgan, an independent designer and developer who believes the best work starts with listening.

THE SHORT VERSION

Good design is a way of paying attention.

I started out making little websites for friends, and kept following the questions that interested me: What makes something feel clear? How do we make the complicated feel human? What does it mean to make something with care?

Today, I work with people who want to make a positive difference. I help them find their story, express it with confidence, and bring it to life on the web.

When I’m away from my desk, I’m usually walking a forest trail, making a very ambitious dinner, or hunting down a used bookstore.

WHAT I BELIEVE

Make it useful.
Make it human.

A few principles I bring into every collaboration, big or small.

01

Listen before making.

The best solution is rarely the loudest one. I take time to understand the people and purpose behind the brief.

02

Clarity is a kindness.

Good design helps people find their way. I aim for intuitive experiences and straightforward communication.

03

Care is in the details.

Accessibility, performance, and small moments of delight all matter. Thoughtful work is work that lasts.
